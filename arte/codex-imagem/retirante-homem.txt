CHARACTER: a drought refugee (retirante) from the backlands of Ceará, about 45 years old, light brown sun-burnt skin, very thin, tired face, short stubble, big hands and big feet. Long-sleeved light cotton shirt buttoned up, with NO pockets, and long loose light cotton trousers with no visible pockets and no belt loops, both a single faded dusty color. The clothes are old, worn thin and covered in dry earth dust, with a few neatly sewn patches at the elbow and knee; hems intact, NOT shredded, NOT torn into rags: a poor man who still mends and washes his clothes. Worn leather sandals (alpercatas) with straps. Wide-brimmed palm straw hat, battered but whole.
